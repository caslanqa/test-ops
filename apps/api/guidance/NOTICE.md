# Notices for the built-in guidance

The built-in guidance packs in `builtin/` are short texts that ship with TestOps. Two of them (`case-design-principles`,
`requirement-review-checklist`) are written for TestOps. The others are adapted, in the TestOps authors' own words and much
shortened, from skills in ECC (https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC), which is licensed under the MIT License:

- `playwright-e2e`, adapted from the `e2e-testing` skill
- `tdd-for-test-code`, adapted from the `tdd-workflow` skill
- `regression-coverage`, adapted from the `ai-regression-testing` skill
- `browser-qa-checks`, adapted from the `browser-qa` skill
